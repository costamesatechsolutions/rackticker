Home Assistant

Connect through MQTT

RackTicker appears in Home Assistant as a device: a light for on/off and brightness, a screen picker, a Show … switch for each screen, Next and Message. Expose the switches to Alexa or Google through Home Assistant (or its Matter bridge) and say “turn on Show Sportsbook”. Needs the Mosquitto broker add-on, or any MQTT broker.
